Privacy

    
Nothing leaves your Mac

    
Clipboard content stays on your Mac. History and captured images are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, using a key held in the macOS Keychain.

    

      

        
Password managers are skipped

        
Items that apps mark as confidential are never recorded, and you can block specific apps entirely.

      

      

        
Signed automatic updates

        
Sparkle checks the public MinutesBack release feed. Every downloaded update must pass an EdDSA signature check before installation.

      

      

        
Safe upgrade from 1.1

        
Snipbird encrypts older history and images automatically, verifies the result, then removes the plaintext originals.
